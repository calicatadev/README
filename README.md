# Hi, we're Calicata 👋

**We build digital systems that generate business.**

## 🦀 What we do

- **Rust engineering:** high-performance backends, services and tooling. Any framework; we don't play favourites (okay, maybe a little).
- **Cybersecurity:** hardening, secure architecture and post-quantum cryptography, because "someday" for quantum computers is a deadline, not an excuse.
- **Web3 & blockchain:** smart contracts, tokens and enterprise blockchain for traceability. Less hype, more audit trails.
- **Custom SaaS:** we build your SaaS from an idea, or take an existing one and make it faster and much harder to break.
- **Web that sells:** Astro, Next.js, React, and custom WordPress child themes when that's the right tool.
- **Brand & design:** so the thing looks as good as it runs.

## 🔐 Security is a team sport

We believe security gets better when more eyes are on it, as long as those eyes say something *before* posting on social media.

- Found a vulnerability in something of ours? **Please tell us privately.** See our [Security Policy](SECURITY.md). You'll get credit and our sincere gratitude. We may also send a meme.
- We report what we find in open source projects responsibly, to the maintainers first.
- We contribute fixes, not just issues titled "this is broken lol".
- We'd rather collaborate with researchers than fight them. Same side, different terminals.

## 🧭 How we work

Our method is called **Código Palanca** (Spanish for "leverage code"):
Diagnosis → Positioning → Strategy → Acquisition → Build → Pre-launch testing & security → Launch & growth.

Security isn't a final checkbox. It has its own phase, and it shows up in every other one.
